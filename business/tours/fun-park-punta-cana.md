# Fun Park Punta Cana – Zip, Ride, Splash & Feast

## Overview

Enjoy an all-in-one adventure experience in Punta Cana with Tours Point RD.

Fun Park Punta Cana combines multiple activities into a single excursion, including ziplining, buggy riding, horseback riding, swimming in an artificial cenote, and enjoying a Dominican meal.

This full-day adventure is designed for guests who want to experience adrenaline, nature, and Dominican culture in one day.
