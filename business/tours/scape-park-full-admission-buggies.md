## Included Attractions

### Getaway Wheels

An off-road buggy adventure through Scape Park's tropical landscape. The route includes narrow dirt trails, water puddles, and muddy terrain.

### Blue Hole Cenote Trail

A scenic trail through varied ecosystems, wetlands, a hanging bridge, waterfalls, and a hidden cenote.

### Cultural Route

A nature and cultural walk featuring replicas of homes associated with Taíno Indigenous communities, Spanish conquistadors, enslaved Africans, and Dominican peasants.

### Saltos Azules

An aquatic adventure featuring the Mabo rope, Macuya Swing, monkey bars, and water hammocks surrounded by caves and waterfalls.

### Zip Line Eco Splash

An aquatic zip-line experience combining aerial adventure with water fountains, cascades, and water cannons.

### Cave Expedition

An underground adventure through chambers and tunnels featuring geological formations.

### Monkey Playground

Controlled open spaces where guests can observe parrots, capuchin monkeys, and rhinoceros iguanas.

### Rope Runners

An aerial challenge course through the treetops featuring suspended ropes and obstacles.

### Super Ride Zip Line

A zip-line attraction designed to create the sensation of flying through the air.

## Typical Experience

Guests arrive at Scape Park and spend the day exploring the included attractions and participating in the Getaway Wheels buggy experience.

The buggy portion takes place on off-road jungle trails with dirt, water, and muddy terrain. Guests should expect to get wet and dirty during the ride.

The remaining time can be spent exploring the park's natural and adventure attractions according to the day's schedule.

## Requirements and Restrictions

### Buggy Requirements

- Minimum age to participate: 10 years old.
- Minimum age to drive: 18 years old.
- Drivers must present a valid driver's license.
- Guests under the minimum participation age cannot join the buggy activity.

### General Park Restrictions

- These activities are not suitable for pregnant women or guests with major illnesses or disabilities.
- Guests under the influence of alcohol or toxic substances cannot participate.
- Natural trails may be difficult for strollers and guests with mobility limitations.
- Zip-line activities have a maximum weight limit of 280 lb.
- Minimum weight for most zip-line activities: 60 lb.
- Minimum weight for the Super Ride: 100 lb.
- Maximum height for zip-line activities: 6 ft 5 in.
- Minimum height for zip-line activities: 4 ft.
- Professional photo and video equipment is not permitted.
- Drones are not permitted.
