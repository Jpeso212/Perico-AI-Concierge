# El Limón Waterfall & Cayo Levantado from Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A full-day excursion from Punta Cana to the Samaná Peninsula combining a luxury catamaran ride, horseback riding through tropical vegetation, a visit to El Limón Waterfall, a Dominican countryside lunch, and free time at Cayo Levantado, also known as Bacardi Island.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Samaná Peninsula, Dominican Republic

Duration: Approximately 12 hours

## Typical Itinerary

Subject to change without notice.

- Depart Punta Cana by air-conditioned transportation.
- Travel through the countryside and along the coastal route toward Las Cañitas.
- Board the luxury catamaran.
- Travel across Samaná Bay toward the peninsula.
- Transfer by safari truck toward the El Limón area.
- Ride horseback through tropical vegetation, coffee, cacao, and fruit plantations.
- Visit El Limón Waterfall.
- Enjoy lunch at a typical countryside ranch.
- Return to Samaná.
- Board the catamaran toward Cayo Levantado.
- Enjoy free time to swim, relax, and explore the island.
- Return to Las Cañitas and continue transportation to Punta Cana.
