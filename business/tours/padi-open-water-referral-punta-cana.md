# PRODUCT SUMMARY

PADI Open Water Referral is designed for students who have already completed the first two portions of their PADI Open Water Diver training with another PADI instructor:

1. Knowledge development / theory
2. Confined-water training

The student comes to Punta Cana to complete the remaining:

4 open-water certification dives.

The current supplier program takes approximately:

2 days.

After successfully completing the required open-water dives and instructor evaluations, the student's PADI Open Water Diver certification is processed.

This is NOT a complete Open Water course from the beginning.

It is the final open-water portion of an existing PADI Open Water training program.

# WHO THIS PRODUCT IS FOR

This product is specifically for students who have already completed:

- PADI knowledge development / theory
AND
- Confined-water training

with the required instructor documentation.

The customer must provide appropriate referral documentation.

This may include:

- Signed PADI Referral Form

OR

- PADI eLearning completion record
PLUS
- Confined-water training sign-off from their instructor

The dive center verifies the documentation before the first dive.

---

# WHO THIS PRODUCT IS NOT FOR

Do NOT route a complete beginner who has done no training to Open Water Referral.

If customer has completed:

NOTHING:
→ Consider full PADI Open Water Diver.

ONLY THEORY / eLearning:
→ Consider PADI Open Water eLearning completion product.

THEORY + CONFINED WATER:
→ Consider PADI Open Water Referral.

ONLY WANTS TO TRY SCUBA:
→ Discover Scuba Diving.

LIMITED TIME AND WANTS ENTRY-LEVEL CERTIFICATION:
→ Compare PADI Scuba Diver.

- Knowledge development
- Confined-water training

OPEN WATER REFERRAL:

- Confined-water training

- 4 open-water certification dives

## Referral vs Open Water eLearning

OPEN WATER eLEARNING PRODUCT:

Customer has completed theory/eLearning.

Still needs:

- Confined-water training
- 4 open-water dives

OPEN WATER REFERRAL:

Customer completed:

- Theory/eLearning
AND
- Confined-water training

Only needs:

- 4 open-water certification dives

This distinction is critical.
