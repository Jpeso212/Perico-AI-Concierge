## Product Summary
Private deep-sea fishing charter operated by Gone Fishing Punta Cana.

The current supplier information establishes a maximum of 10 total passengers, with a maximum of 7 fishermen. Remaining guests may participate as companions.

The charter includes transportation within the confirmed Bavaro–Punta Cana service area, fishing equipment, bait, beverages, crew and guide support.

# MARINE AND WEATHER CONDITIONS

Deep-sea fishing is subject to marine and weather safety conditions.

Rain alone does not automatically cancel the charter.

Unsafe sea conditions, excessive wind, tropical weather or other marine safety conditions may result in:

- Cancellation
- Postponement
- Rescheduling
- Route changes
- Operational modifications

The captain/operator has final authority regarding safe operation at sea.

---

# SEASICKNESS

Deep-sea fishing can involve significant boat movement.

Guests, particularly families with children, should understand that seasickness is possible.

Once the charter has departed, seasickness does not automatically create a refund entitlement.

If guests decide to proceed and later request an early return because of seasickness, do not promise a full or partial refund.

Route, continuation, shortening and return decisions at sea remain operational decisions of the captain/operator.
