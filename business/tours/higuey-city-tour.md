## Typical Experience

Guests are picked up from their hotel and travel to Higüey, one of the most important cities in eastern Dominican Republic.

The tour begins with a visit to the Basilica of La Altagracia, the country's largest and most important religious monument. Guests learn about the basilica's history, architecture, and importance to Dominican culture.

The experience continues through the city center, where guests observe local cafeterias, fruit stalls, vendors, monuments, and everyday Dominican life.

Guests also visit St. Dionysius Church and explore historic areas connected to Higüey's religious and cultural heritage.

The excursion includes a visit where guests can observe traditional cigar-making techniques and learn about the role of tobacco in the Dominican economy.

The tour concludes with opportunities to purchase local handicrafts, cigars, Dominican rum, and other traditional products before returning to Punta Cana.

## Main Attractions

### Basilica of La Altagracia

The Basilica of La Altagracia is one of the most important religious landmarks in the Dominican Republic and a major pilgrimage destination.

### St. Dionysius Church

A historic church associated with Higüey's religious heritage and more than 500 years of local history.

### Higüey City Center

Guests experience the city's streets, monuments, restaurants, cafeterias, marketplace, and everyday activities.

### Local Cigar-Making

Guests learn how traditional cigars are handmade and discover the historical importance of tobacco production in the Dominican Republic.

### Local Handicrafts and Rum

The excursion provides opportunities to purchase traditional Dominican crafts, costume jewelry, rum, cigars, and other local products.
