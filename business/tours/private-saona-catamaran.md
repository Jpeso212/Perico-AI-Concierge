# PRODUCT SUMMARY

The Private Saona Island Catamaran is a private full-day experience operated and managed by Perico Ripiao Tours.

The experience combines:

- Private catamaran
- Snorkeling
- Two Natural Pool visits
- Paradise Beach
- Lunch
- Drinks
- Snacks
- Music onboard
- Private group atmosphere

The catamaran is reserved exclusively for the customer's group.

This product must not be confused with:

- Private Saona Speedboat
- Shared Saona excursions
- Shared catamaran excursions
