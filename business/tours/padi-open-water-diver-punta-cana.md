# COURSE STRUCTURE

## 1. PADI eLearning / Knowledge Development

The course includes knowledge development.

PADI eLearning allows students to complete theory online before arriving in Punta Cana.

The supplier references subjects including:

- Dive physics
- Scuba equipment
- Safety procedures
- Dive planning

Students may work through the online material at their own pace.

When the student arrives, the instructor reviews the material and answers questions before in-water training.

---

## 2. Confined-Water Training

Students complete training in a confined-water environment.

The supplier states that complete scuba equipment is included.

Equipment referenced includes:

- Wetsuit
- BCD
- Regulator
- Mask
- Fins

Students learn how to use the equipment before progressing to open water.

Supplier references skills including:

## Open Water vs PADI Scuba Diver

PADI Scuba Diver:

- 2 days
- 2 open-water dives
- Maximum 12 m / 40 ft
- Professional supervision required after certification
- Can later upgrade

PADI Open Water Diver:

- 3 days
- 4 open-water dives
- Maximum 18 m / 60 ft
- Certified buddy diving within applicable limits

If vacation time is limited:
Compare both options factually.

Do not describe one as universally better.
