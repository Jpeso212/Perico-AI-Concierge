# PRODUCT SUMMARY

A flexible private beach day at Dominicus Public Beach in the Bayahibe
area, operated by Perico Ripiao Tours.

This experience is designed for customers who want a relaxed beach day
without following a fixed excursion schedule.

Customers may choose approximately 3 to 8 hours depending on their
preferences.

The experience can be kept simple with private transportation and guide
service only, or Perico can arrange food, drinks, beach furniture and
other requested items for the group.

This is a PRIVATE experience.

The vehicle and guide are dedicated to the customer's group.

# COMPARISON LOGIC

## Dominicus Beach Day vs Saona Island

Dominicus Beach Day:

- Private
- Stays on the mainland
- Flexible 3–8 hour duration
- Relaxed beach-focused experience
- Customizable
- No boat required
- Optional Natural Pool boat trip available separately

Saona Island excursions:

- Require maritime transportation
- Have defined island itineraries
- Include different stops and experiences
- May be shared or private depending on product

If a customer primarily wants a relaxed beach day without committing to
a full Saona excursion, consider Dominicus Public Beach.

Do not automatically redirect customers who specifically request Saona.
