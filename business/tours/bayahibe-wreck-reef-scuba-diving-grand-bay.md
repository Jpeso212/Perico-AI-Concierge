# PRODUCT SUMMARY

Bayahibe Wreck & Reef Scuba Diving is a diving excursion from Punta Cana to the Caribbean coast of Bayahibe.

The excursion is designed for certified divers and currently includes:

- Hotel transportation from eligible Punta Cana / Bávaro hotels
- Ground transportation to Bayahibe
- 2 guided scuba dives
- Complete scuba equipment
- Professional dive guidance
- Wreck and/or reef diving

Bayahibe offers multiple reef and wreck sites.

The supplier highlights:

- St. George Wreck
- Atlantic Princess
- Coral gardens
- Reef sites
- Deeper wall environments

- Certification

# ST. GEORGE CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENT

Supplier currently states:

Advanced Open Water certification is required to dive St. George.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE:

The wreck reaches approximately:

40 m / 131 ft.

Standard PADI Advanced Open Water certification has a normal recreational certification depth of:

30 m / 100 ft.

Therefore:

AI must NEVER interpret "Advanced Open Water required" as automatic authorization for every diver to descend to 40 m / 131 ft.

The actual dive profile must follow:

- Diver certification
- Additional training
- Diver experience
- Instructor/divemaster decision
- Applicable PADI standards
- Safety requirements

If the planned profile approaches depths beyond the diver's certification limits, additional qualification may be required.

AI must not independently authorize a 40-meter dive.
