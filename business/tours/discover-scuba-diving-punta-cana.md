# PRODUCT SUMMARY

Discover Scuba Diving is an introductory PADI scuba-diving experience designed for customers who have never scuba dived before and are not certified divers.

No previous scuba-diving certification is required.

The experience includes:

- Introductory theory briefing
- Basic scuba skills instruction
- Shallow-water skills practice
- One open-water dive
- Direct supervision by a PADI professional
- Complete scuba equipment
- Hotel transportation
- Small-group format

The maximum diving depth is:

6 meters / 20 feet.

The current supplier lists the total experience at approximately:

2.5 hours.

# EXPERIENCE STRUCTURE

## 1. Theory Briefing

Before entering the water, participants receive an introductory scuba briefing.

The briefing covers basic diving concepts and safety information.

---

## 2. Basic Skills Practice

Participants practice basic scuba-diving skills in shallow water.

Supplier references skills including:

- Breathing through the regulator
- Clearing the mask
- Basic underwater hand signals

The instructor determines progression according to safety and participant comfort.

---

## 3. Open-Water Dive

After completing the introductory instruction and skills portion, participants complete one open-water dive.

Maximum depth:

6 meters / 20 feet.

The participant remains under direct supervision of a PADI professional.

- Formal certification course
- Multi-stage training
