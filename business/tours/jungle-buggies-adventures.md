# Jungle Buggies Adventures Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A half-day jungle buggy adventure through the Anamuya Mountains, combining off-road driving, a swim in the Anamuya River, and a visit to a typical Dominican home and coffee plantation.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Anamuya Mountains / Punta Cana region, Dominican Republic

Duration: Approximately 5 hours

## Pricing

Prices are per Honda Pioneer 700 buggy:

- 1 person: US$139
- 2 people: US$179
- 3 people: US$249
- 4 people: US$289

Prices and availability must be confirmed before booking.
