## PRODUCT SUMMARY

Polaris VIP is a premium off-road experience at Bávaro Adventure Park.

Guests ride a Polaris or Can-Am vehicle through the park's exclusive Jungle Route and an off-road track located in an abandoned mine.

The experience also includes the Blue Lagoon Cenote, Dominican Village, Waterfall Pool, Maze Garden, and a traditional Dominican lunch.

Vehicles accommodate up to 4 people.

### 1. Polaris / Can-Am Off-Road Adventure

Guests ride a Polaris or Can-Am vehicle through Bávaro Adventure Park's exclusive Jungle Route.

### 2. Blue Lagoon Cenote

Guests visit the Blue Lagoon Cenote for an opportunity to swim and cool off in freshwater.

### 3. Dominican Village

Guests visit the park's Dominican Village and learn about elements of traditional Dominican rural culture.

---

### 4. Waterfall Pool

Guests can relax and swim at the Waterfall Pool.

---

### 5. Maze Garden

Access to the Maze Garden is included.

---

### 6. Dominican Lunch

A typical Dominican lunch is included.
