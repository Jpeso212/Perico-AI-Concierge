# Private Catalina Island Tour

## Product Overview

Private Catalina Island is a full-day private excursion from Punta Cana and surrounding hotel areas to Catalina Island, departing from Bayahibe.

The experience combines a private transportation service, boat travel, snorkeling, beach relaxation, lunch, beverages, and island entertainment.

The tour is designed for families, couples, friends, and private groups who want a more personalized experience and greater flexibility than a shared excursion.

## Main Itinerary

The itinerary may be adjusted according to the group's selected pickup time, operational conditions, and sea conditions.

### 1. Hotel Pickup

Guests are picked up directly from their hotel at a time selected for their booking.

The exact pickup time depends on the group's preferences and operational feasibility.

### 2. Transfer to Bayahibe

Guests travel by vehicle from their hotel to Bayahibe.

Upon arrival, the team provides a briefing and guests put on their life jackets before boarding the boat.

### 3. Boat Departure

The group departs from Bayahibe toward Catalina Island.

The boat ride is part of the private excursion experience.

### 4. Snorkeling at The Wall

Before arriving at Catalina Island, the first snorkeling stop takes place at The Wall.

Guests generally spend approximately 45 minutes snorkeling, subject to weather, sea, and safety conditions.

Snorkeling equipment is included.

### 5. Catalina Island

After snorkeling, the group continues to Catalina Island.

Guests can enjoy the beach, relax in beach chairs, and spend time with the included beverages and island animation.

### 6. Lunch

Lunch is served on Catalina Island around 12:30 PM.

The typical lunch includes:

- Grilled chicken.
- Rice.
- Pork chops.
- Colored rice.
- Pasta with red sauce.
- Salad.
- Fruits.
- Bread.
- Coffee.

The exact menu may vary according to operational availability.

### 7. Second Snorkeling at The Aquarium

Guests may choose to participate in a second snorkeling experience at The Aquarium.

This second snorkeling activity is included in the tour price.

Guests who prefer not to snorkel a second time may remain on the island and enjoy the afternoon with beverages and beach relaxation.

### 8. Return to Bayahibe

The group must be ready to leave Catalina Island by 4:00 PM.

This is the firm departure cutoff for the return trip to Bayahibe.

### 9. Return to Hotel

Upon arrival in Bayahibe, the driver waits for the group and provides transportation back to the hotel.

## Private vs. Shared Catalina

Perico Ripiao Tours offers both Private Catalina and Shared Catalina excursions.

### Private Catalina

The private option is designed for guests who want a more personalized experience.

Benefits include:

- Private transportation for the group.
- A more personalized service.
- Greater flexibility regarding pickup time.
- More control over the pace of the experience, subject to operational feasibility.
- No need to travel with unrelated guests in the same private arrangement.

### Shared Catalina

The shared option includes transportation and activities with other customers.

Benefits include:

- A generally lower price.
- A scheduled group itinerary.
- Shared transportation and activities.

### AI Sales Guidance

When comparing the two options, the AI should explain that:

- Private is best for guests who value privacy, flexibility, and personalized attention.
- Shared is best for guests who prefer a more economical group experience.
- Neither option should be described as better for every customer.
- The recommendation should depend on the group's budget, size, preferences, and desired level of flexibility.

The AI must not promise exclusive access, unlimited beverages, or itinerary changes unless specifically confirmed by the operations team.
