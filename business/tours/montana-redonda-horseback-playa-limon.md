Montaña Redonda + Horseback Riding Playa Limón is a half-day shared excursion combining panoramic mountain scenery with a coastal horseback-riding experience in Miches.

- Famous swings and hammocks
- Playa Limón
- Horseback riding along the coast
- Beach time
- Opportunity to swim
- Coconut
- Rum
- Cola
- Water

This product is especially appropriate for customers looking for a combination of nature, photography, horseback riding and beach scenery.
