# Adventure ATV Drive Punta Cana

## Overview

Experience an adrenaline-filled half-day ATV or quad adventure through the Dominican countryside in Macao, Punta Cana.

Drive your own vehicle along private muddy off-road trails before visiting Domitai Park and its private cenote, Cueva Chiquita. Enjoy a refreshing swim in the crystal-clear waters and discover traditional Dominican organic products through a tasting experience featuring coffee, chocolate, tea, mamajuana, and more.

The adventure also includes free time in a Dominican village where guests can browse local products and souvenirs.
