# PRODUCT SUMMARY

PADI Open Water eLearning Punta Cana is designed for students who have already purchased PADI Open Water eLearning directly from PADI and completed their knowledge development online.

The customer arrives in Punta Cana with the theory already completed.

Grand Bay then provides the remaining practical training:

- Quick theory review
- Confined-water training
- Fundamental scuba skills
- 4 open-water certification dives
- Complete scuba equipment
- Boat trips
- PADI instructor
- Certification processing
- Hotel transportation
- Small-group instruction

After successfully completing all required practical training, dives, skills and instructor evaluations, the student's PADI Open Water Diver certification is processed.
