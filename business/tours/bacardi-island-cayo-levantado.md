# Bacardi Island (Cayo Levantado)

## Product Overview

A full-day excursion from Punta Cana to Samaná Bay and Cayo Levantado, also known as Bacardi Island. Guests travel by air-conditioned vehicle and catamaran to enjoy a natural pool, turquoise waters, white-sand beaches, tropical scenery, and free time on the island.

## Typical Experience

Guests are picked up from their hotel and transported to the Samaná area. From there, they board a catamaran and travel across Samaná Bay.

The excursion includes a stop at a natural pool, where guests can swim and enjoy the clear Caribbean waters. The journey continues to Cayo Levantado, where guests can relax on the beach, swim, sunbathe, and enjoy views of the surrounding mountains.

The day includes lunch and drinks before returning to Punta Cana.
