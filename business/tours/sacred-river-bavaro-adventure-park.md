## PRODUCT SUMMARY

Sacred River is a nature-focused experience at Bávaro Adventure Park featuring an underground river, freshwater cenote, jungle river, waterfall pool, Dominican cultural experience, and traditional Dominican lunch.

This product is designed for travelers looking for a combination of nature, swimming, relaxation, culture, and light adventure rather than a high-adrenaline excursion.

## MAIN EXPERIENCES

### 1. Sacred River

Explore an underground river located inside Bávaro Adventure Park.

The experience passes through crystal-clear freshwater, natural limestone formations, tunnels, and semi-enclosed and open cenotes.

---

### 2. Blue Cenote Lagoon

Guests can enjoy a freshwater open cenote surrounded by tropical vegetation.

This portion of the experience is suitable for swimming and relaxing.

---

### 3. Jungle River Adventure

Guests float through a tropical environment on tubes while following a gentle current.

The experience includes tropical vegetation, water features, and a cave environment.

---

### 4. Waterfall Pool

Guests can swim and relax in the park's waterfall pool surrounded by tropical scenery.

---

### 5. Maze Garden

Access to the Maze Garden is included with this product.

---

### 6. Dominican Village

Guests visit a recreation of a traditional Dominican village and learn about Dominican rural life and culture.

The experience includes information about traditional lifestyles and local products.

Coffee and cocoa sampling may be available during the visit.

---

### 7. Dominican Lunch

A traditional Dominican lunch is included.

Supplier currently describes the meal as including:

- Rice
- Beans
- Oven-roasted chicken
- Creole-style sauce

Drinks with lunch are NOT included.
