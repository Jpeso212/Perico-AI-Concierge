# Bavaro Runners Culture Tour

## Product Overview

A full-day Dominican countryside and cultural experience combining transportation by safari truck, local traditions, rural landscapes, Dominican cuisine, and a visit to Macao Beach.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Punta Cana, Higüey, and surrounding Dominican countryside

Duration: Approximately 8 hours

## Pricing

- Adult ages 13+: US$90 per person
- Child ages 3–12: US$45 per person
- Infant: Free
- Early booking promotion: Use code EARLY10 for 10% off when booking 10 or more days in advance, subject to supplier terms and availability.

## Main Highlights

- Travel through the Dominican countryside in a safari-style truck.
- Visit the Basilica Catedral Nuestra Señora de la Altagracia in Higüey.
- Drive through sugar cane and rice plantations.
- Learn about traditional sugar cane processing using a trapiche.
- See how sugar cane juice is transformed into molasses and other products.
- Learn about Dominican tobacco and cigar rolling.
- Visit a typical Dominican family home and farm.
- Learn about local coffee and cacao production.
- Taste fresh sugar cane juice, coffee, cacao, and tropical fruits.
- Enjoy a typical Dominican buffet lunch.
- Visit Macao Beach.
- Experience rural landscapes and local communities.
