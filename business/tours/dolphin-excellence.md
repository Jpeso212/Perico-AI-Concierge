# Dolphin Excellence — Swim with 2 Dolphins

## Overview

Dolphin Excellence is an advanced dolphin interaction experience at Dolphin Explorer in Punta Cana, Dominican Republic.

Guests swim and interact with two dolphins during a guided marine-life experience.

## Pricing

- Adult, age 13+: US$199 per person
- Child, ages 1–12: US$120 per person

Prices are subject to change and availability.

## Duration

- Approximately 2 hours total.
- Includes approximately 60 minutes in the water.
- Guests should arrive 30 minutes before the scheduled program.

## Schedule

Available daily, subject to availability.

Typical sessions may include:

- 9:30 AM
- 11:30 AM
- 2:45 PM

Confirm the schedule before booking.

## Location

Dolphin Explorer
Cabeza de Toro Street
Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

## Experience Includes

- Guided interaction with two dolphins
- Swimming with two dolphins
- Educational information about dolphin behavior and conservation
- Approximately 60 minutes of water activities

## Group Information

- The program operates in groups.
- Dolphin assignment and group size are subject to operator availability.

## Age and Swimming Requirements

- Minimum age: 1 year old.
- Children under 8 years old must be accompanied by an adult.
- Swimming ability requirements must be confirmed with the operator before booking.
- Children under 1 meter tall may swim free when accompanied by a paying adult, subject to operator terms.

## Pregnancy and Health

- Pregnancy policies must be confirmed with the operator before booking.
- Guests with medical conditions, mobility limitations, or disabilities should consult the operator before participating.

## What to Bring

- Swimsuit
- Towel
- Biodegradable sunscreen
- Extra cash for optional photos or personal expenses
