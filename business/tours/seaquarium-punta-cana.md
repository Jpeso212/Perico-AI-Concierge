# Seaquarium Punta Cana

## Overview

Seaquarium Punta Cana is a shared ocean adventure combining an underwater helmet walk, marine encounters, snorkeling, a Caribbean party aboard the boat, a natural pool stop, and time at a private beach.

## Highlights

- Underwater helmet walk / Sea Walk
- Caribbean party onboard
- Sea lion encounter
- Snorkeling with sharks and rays
- Natural pool
- Private beach
- Snacks
- Round-trip transportation from applicable hotel areas

## Underwater Helmet Walk / Sea Walk

The underwater helmet experience is one of the main attractions of Seaquarium.

Guests walk underwater using a special helmet that allows them to breathe normally while submerged.

### Height Restriction

Guests under approximately **120 cm / 3 ft 11 in** cannot participate in the helmet dive.

If a child's height is uncertain, verify eligibility before promising participation.

### Pregnancy

Pregnant guests may participate in the excursion, but they cannot participate in the underwater helmet dive / Sea Walk.

Do not tell a pregnant guest that the entire excursion is prohibited solely because of pregnancy.

## Snorkeling

Snorkeling with sharks and rays is part of the experience.

Guests may bring their own snorkeling equipment if desired.

Participation remains subject to operator safety instructions and conditions on the day.

Do not guarantee specific animal behavior or underwater conditions.

## Natural Pool

The excursion includes a natural pool experience.

Do not confuse this with the Saona Island Natural Pool.
