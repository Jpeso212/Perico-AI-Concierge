# Seaquarium Punta Cana

## Overview

Experience an exciting ocean adventure in Punta Cana combining an underwater helmet walk, marine encounters, snorkeling, a Caribbean party aboard the boat, and time at a private beach. Seaquarium is an approximately three-hour excursion available daily with two departures.

## Highlights

- Walk underwater using a special helmet.
- Enjoy a Caribbean party onboard.
- Experience a sea lion encounter.
- Snorkel with sharks and rays.
- Relax at a private beach.
- Enjoy included snacks.
- Round-trip transportation included.
