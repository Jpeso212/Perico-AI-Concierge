# River Ranch Explorer

## Overview

Explore the Dominican countryside on an exciting 4x4 ATV or off-road buggy adventure through private trails near Punta Cana.

This cultural and nature experience combines adrenaline, scenic countryside routes, a refreshing swim in a natural stream, Taíno history, Dominican cooking, local tastings, and relaxation at a rustic river ranch.

Guests visit the sacred La Ceiba tree, learn about its importance to the Taíno people, stop at a traditional Dominican country house, participate in a hands-on cooking experience, and enjoy a swim in a crystal-clear Caribbean river oasis.

## Itinerary

### Morning Schedule

- 7:30–8:30 AM: Pickup from hotels in Bávaro and Punta Cana using support buses.
- 9:00 AM: Arrival at the river ranch.
- 9:15 AM: Safety briefing and explanation of the quad bikes and buggies.
- 9:30 AM: Departure from the ranch and start of the countryside trail.
- 10:00 AM: Arrival at the natural stream for a short stop.
- 10:30 AM: Drive to the traditional country house.
- 10:45 AM: Arrival at the river and country house area, including a quick dip and cooking experience.
- 11:30 AM: Departure from the river on the quads or buggies.
- 11:45 AM: Arrival at the ranch for snacks and pool time.
- 12:45 PM: Departure for hotel drop-offs.
