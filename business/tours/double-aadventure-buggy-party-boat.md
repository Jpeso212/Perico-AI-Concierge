## PRODUCT SUMMARY

Double AAdventure is a full-day combination experience that brings together land and sea activities in Punta Cana.

The experience begins with the Buggy VIP adventure at Bávaro Adventure Park, including off-road driving, Blue Lagoon Cenote, Dominican Village, Waterfall Pool and a traditional Dominican lunch.

Guests then continue with the Adventure Explorer Catamaran for approximately 3 hours of sailing along the Bávaro coastline with snorkeling, unlimited national drinks, snacks, music and entertainment.

This product is designed for customers who want both an off-road adventure and a Caribbean boat experience in the same day.
