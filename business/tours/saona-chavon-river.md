## Product Summary

Shared full-day excursion combining the Chavón River with Saona Island.

The experience travels by speedboat and combines river scenery, Palmilla
Sandbank, protected mangroves, Catuano Canal, a tropical Saona Island
beach and the Natural Pools.

This is a separate product from:

- Shared Saona Speedboat
- Shared Saona Catamaran
- Private Saona Speedboat
- Private Saona Catamaran

Do not merge their itineraries, prices or policies.

---

## Experience Type

Shared full-day excursion.

Main transportation on the water:
Speedboat.

This is NOT a catamaran excursion.

## Main Experiences

Typical itinerary includes:

1. Hotel pickup from eligible areas
2. Ground transportation to Bayahibe
3. Speedboat departure
4. Chavón River navigation
5. Palmilla Sandbank
6. Protected mangrove area
7. Catuano Canal
8. Saona Island tropical beach
9. Natural Pools
10. Return by speedboat

## Chavón River

The excursion includes navigation through the Chavón River.

The river is known for its tropical vegetation, cliffs and landscapes.

observed.

## Mobility & Health Restrictions

This excursion is not suitable for:

- Wheelchair users
- Guests with very limited mobility

It is not recommended for guests with severe:

- back problems
- hip problems
- knee problems

The excursion requires customers to:

- board and exit a speedboat
- walk on sand
- tolerate a full-day outdoor excursion

confirmation.

## Cancellation Policy

### Customer Cancellation

Current supplier-specific policy:

Customer receives a 75% refund when cancellation notice is provided
before 5:00 PM on the day before the excursion.

Do not promise a refund for later cancellations unless specifically
authorized.

### Weather / Operator Cancellation

The excursion normally operates with light rain.

If weather or sea conditions make navigation unsafe and the operator
cancels the excursion, customers may be offered:

- Rescheduling to another available date
OR
- Full refund of the amount paid

The final operational decision is made based on maritime and safety
conditions.

This weather policy applies when the operator cancels for safety
reasons.

Do not automatically apply this policy to other Perico excursions.
