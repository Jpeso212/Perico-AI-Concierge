# Jungle Buggies + Zipline Adventure Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A full-day jungle adventure combining a Honda Pioneer 700 buggy experience, swimming in the Anamuya River, a visit to a typical Dominican home, and a 12-cable zipline circuit through the tropical mountains.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Anamuya Mountains / Punta Cana region, Dominican Republic

Duration: Approximately 8 hours

## Zipline Information

- 12 cables across 18 platforms.
- Course duration approximately 1–1.5 hours.
- Cable #6 may reach approximately 55 km/h.
- Cable #9 is the highest cable.
- Cable #11 is approximately 800 meters long and operates side by side.
- The zipline circuit is certified by ACCT.

## Age Requirements

- Minimum age: 6 years old.
- Adults: Ages 13–90.
- Buggy driver must be at least 18 years old.
- Children ages 6–12 only participate up to zipline #8.
- Children must be accompanied by an adult.

## Buggy Driver Requirements

- Minimum age: 18 years old.
- Valid driver's license required.
- At least one year of driving experience required.

## Physical Requirements

- Minimum weight: 20 kg / 44 lb.
- Maximum weight: 127 kg / 280 lb.
- Maximum waist measurement: 112 cm / 44 in.
- Covered ankle shoes or sandals required.
- Flip-flops are not permitted.

## Restrictions

- Pregnant guests cannot participate.
- Guests with heart disease cannot participate.
- Guests with disabilities or mobility problems cannot participate.
- Guests with mobility limitations should not participate.
- Children under 12 years old only complete the first eight zipline cables.
