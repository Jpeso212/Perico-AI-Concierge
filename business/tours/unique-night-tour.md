# Unique Night Tour by Adventure Boogies

## Overview

Discover Punta Cana after dark with an exciting evening adventure at Domitai Park. Ride through private off-road trails, explore Dominican and Taíno culture, swim in an underground cenote with live DJ entertainment, enjoy a traditional Dominican dinner, and finish the night with a lively carnival show under the Caribbean stars.

## Highlights

- One-hour dune buggy adventure on private trails
- Taíno village and ancestral cultural show
- Dominican organic product tasting
- Coffee, chocolate, tea, mamajuana, and more
- Swim in the Iguanaboina underground cenote
- Live DJ atmosphere at the cenote
- Typical Dominican dinner
- Mardi Gras-style carnival show
- Open bar during dinner and the show
- Certified multilingual guides
- Hotel pickup and return transportation

## Itinerary

- **4:30–5:30 PM:** Hotel pickup.
- **5:45 PM:** Arrival at the park, check-in, and safety briefing.
- **6:00 PM:** One-hour dune buggy adventure on private trails.
- **7:00 PM:** Visit to the replica Taíno village and cultural show.
- **7:30 PM:** Organic products tasting experience.
- **8:00 PM:** Party and swim at Iguanaboina cenote with live DJ entertainment.
- **8:30 PM:** Typical Dominican dinner.
- **9:15 PM:** Carnival show with open bar.
- **10:00 PM:** Return transportation to the hotels.

## Important Information

- Drivers must be over 18 years old.
- Family buggies for 3–4 passengers are available upon request.
- Buggies are not suitable for pregnant women.
- Buggies are not suitable for guests with mobility issues or back problems.
- Guests with mobility limitations may join the excursion after the buggy portion.
- Follow all safety instructions provided by the guides.
- Activity schedules may vary.
