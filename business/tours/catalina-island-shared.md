# PRODUCT SUMMARY

Catalina Island Shared is a full-day excursion from the Punta Cana area to Catalina Island.

Guests are transported toward La Romana, where they board the boat for the marine portion of the excursion.

The experience combines snorkeling with beach time at Catalina Island.

Lunch, open bar, beach chairs, snorkeling equipment/life jackets and transportation are included.

Some departures may also carry scuba divers.

When scuba divers are participating, snorkeling guests may continue snorkeling or relax on the boat while the divers complete their underwater activity and resurface.

# SNORKELING & SCUBA COORDINATION

This Shared Catalina excursion may operate on the same boat as scuba-diving guests.

The presence of scuba divers is NOT guaranteed on every departure.

## When Scuba Divers Are On the Trip

Snorkeling guests complete their snorkeling activity.

While scuba divers remain underwater, snorkeling guests may:

- Continue snorkeling, or
- Wait and relax on the boat

Once the scuba divers have resurfaced and returned to the boat, the group can continue with the excursion itinerary.

## When There Are No Scuba Divers

If there are no scuba divers on the departure, this coordination/waiting period does not apply.

The excursion continues according to the normal operating itinerary.

## Concierge Communication Rule

Do NOT tell customers that scuba divers will definitely be on their trip.

When this information is relevant, explain it naturally:

"Some departures may also carry scuba divers. If there are divers on your trip, after your snorkeling you can continue snorkeling or relax on the boat while they finish their dive. Once everyone is back on board, the excursion continues."

Do not describe this as:

- A second guaranteed snorkeling stop
- Additional paid snorkeling
- A separate excursion
- A delay caused by another tour

It is part of the shared boat operation when scuba divers are present.
