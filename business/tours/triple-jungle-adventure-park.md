# Triple Jungle Adventure Park Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A full-day adventure combining a Honda Pioneer 700 buggy, ziplining through the Dominican rainforest, swimming in the Anamuya River, visiting Monkeyland, and experiencing Dominican countryside culture.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Anamuya Mountains / Punta Cana region, Dominican Republic

Duration: Approximately 10 hours

## Pricing

Prices are per Honda Pioneer 700 buggy:

- 1 person: US$209
- 2 people: US$351
- 3 people: US$467
- 4 people: US$553

Prices and availability must be confirmed before booking.

## Age Requirements

- Minimum age: 6 years old.
- Adults: Ages 13–90.
- Buggy driver must be at least 18 years old.
- Children ages 6–12 only participate in the zipline circuit through cable #8.

## Buggy Driver Requirements

- Must be at least 18 years old.
- Must have a valid driver's license.
- Must have at least one year of driving experience.

## Physical Requirements

- Minimum weight: 20 kg / 44 lb.
- Maximum weight: 127 kg / 280 lb.
- Maximum waist measurement: 112 cm / 44 in.
- Covered ankle shoes or sandals are required.
- Flip-flops are not permitted.

## Restrictions

- Pregnant guests cannot participate.
- Guests with heart disease cannot participate.
- Guests with disabilities or mobility problems cannot participate.
- Guests with back problems should confirm suitability before booking.
- Children must be accompanied by an adult.
- A liability waiver may be required.
