# Saona Island Shared Tour

## Tour Overview

The Saona Island Shared Tour is a full-day group excursion operated by Caribbean Saona. The tour operates seven days a week and includes hotel pickup and return transportation, marine transportation, beach time on Saona Island, a natural pool visit, lunch, drinks, and entertainment.

This is a shared group excursion and is not a private tour.

## Provider Information

- Provider: Caribbean Saona
- Tour name: Saona Island Shared Tour
- Tour type: Shared excursion
- Destination: Saona Island, Dominican Republic
- Operating days: 7 days a week
- Website: Not available

## Duration

- Approximate total duration: 11 hours, including transportation.
- Approximate beach time on Saona Island: 3 hours.
- Approximate natural pool visit: 45 minutes.
- Approximate catamaran journey: 2–2.5 hours.

The total duration may vary depending on hotel location, transportation logistics, sea conditions, and the daily operating schedule.

## Marine Transportation

The excursion uses both a catamaran and a motomarán during the marine transportation portion.

The exact arrangement depends on the daily operation.

### Outbound Journey

Guests may travel to Saona Island by either:

- Catamaran; or
- Motomarán.

### Return Journey

The return journey uses the other marine vessel.

Examples:

- Catamaran outbound and motomarán return.
- Motomarán outbound and catamaran return.

The exact vessel assignment is confirmed by Caribbean Saona.

## Marine Journey Time

The catamaran journey takes approximately 2–2.5 hours.

This duration may apply to either direction:

- If the catamaran is used outbound, the outbound catamaran journey takes approximately 2–2.5 hours.
- If the catamaran is used for the return, the return catamaran journey takes approximately 2–2.5 hours.
- The other direction is operated by motomarán.

The marine transportation duration may vary depending on sea conditions and the daily operating arrangement.

The general itinerary is:

1. Hotel pickup.

7. Lunch and drinks.
8. Visit to the natural pool for approximately 45 minutes.

The exact sequence and timing may vary according to the provider's daily operation.
