## PRODUCT SUMMARY

Polaris & Zipline is a premium combination adventure at Bávaro Adventure Park that combines an off-road Polaris or Can-Am experience with the Zipline Mega Splash circuit.

Guests drive a Polaris or Can-Am through an off-road track located inside an abandoned mine and experience the park's 6-line zipline circuit, including the Mega Splash Cable with a water landing.

The package also includes Blue Lagoon Cenote, Waterfall Pool, Maze Garden, Dominican Village and a traditional Dominican lunch.
