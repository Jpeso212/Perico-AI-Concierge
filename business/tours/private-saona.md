# Private Saona Island Tour

## Standard Itinerary

The Private Saona Island Tour operates as a private speedboat experience from the customer's hotel to Bayahibe and back.

The typical route is:

**Hotel → Bayahibe → Natural Pool → Mangroves → Catuano Canal → Canto de la Playa* → Mano Juan* → Paradise Beach → Snorkeling → Natural Pool → Bayahibe → Hotel**

### Standard Stops and Activities

- Bayahibe
- Natural Pool
- Mangroves
- Catuano Canal
- Paradise Beach
- Snorkeling
- Return to Bayahibe

### Optional Stops

**Canto de la Playa** — optional, available upon request, and subject to an additional cost.

**Mano Juan** — optional and subject to itinerary and operational confirmation.

The AI must not present Canto de la Playa or Mano Juan as guaranteed standard stops.

### Weather and Sea Conditions

Snorkeling is part of the experience but is subject to sea and weather conditions.

The exact itinerary or activities may need to be adjusted when weather, sea conditions, safety considerations, or other operational circumstances require changes.

The AI must never guarantee a specific stop or activity when conditions may prevent it.

---

## Canto de la Playa — Optional Experience

Canto de la Playa is **not included in the standard Private Saona Island itinerary**.

It is an optional addition available upon request and carries an **additional cost**.

Canto de la Playa is located in a very remote part of Saona Island. It is the farthest beach that can be visited on the island and is known for being isolated, pristine, and relatively untouched.

Because of the additional travel time involved, adding Canto de la Playa affects the overall itinerary.

### AI Recommendation Rule

The AI should:

- Clearly explain that Canto de la Playa is optional.
- Clearly explain that it carries an additional cost.
- Not imply that Canto de la Playa is included in the standard tour price.
- Consider the customer's group, children, priorities, and available time before recommending it.
- Confirm availability and additional cost before promising the stop.
- Never invent the additional cost.

---

## Mano Juan — Optional Visit

Mano Juan is the only inhabited town on Saona Island.

A visit to Mano Juan can be considered as an optional stop when **Canto de la Playa is included in the tour**.

Because of the additional time involved, Perico Ripiao Tours does not normally encourage or proactively offer Mano Juan when Canto de la Playa is not included.

### AI Recommendation Rule

The AI should:

- Explain that Mano Juan is available as an optional visit primarily in conjunction with Canto de la Playa.
- Not proactively promote Mano Juan as a standalone upgrade when Canto de la Playa is not part of the itinerary.
- If a customer specifically asks about Mano Juan, explain the relationship between the two stops and refer the request to the team for confirmation.
- Never promise the Mano Juan visit without confirming the itinerary and operational feasibility.

### Customer-Facing Explanation

If a customer asks about Mano Juan, the AI can explain:

"Mano Juan is the only inhabited town on Saona Island. We can include a visit when Canto de la Playa is part of your itinerary, since both locations are in the more remote part of the island. If you're interested, we can check whether it can be added to your experience."

## Schedule Flexibility

One of the advantages of the Private Saona Island experience is the flexibility of the itinerary.

Customers can request to:

- Shorten the excursion
- Return to Bayahibe earlier
- Spend less time at certain stops
- Leave later in the day, when operationally possible

The private experience can be adapted to the customer's preferences, subject to the captain's guidance, weather, sea conditions, and operational requirements.

### Mandatory Natural Pool Departure Cutoff

If customers choose to leave later in the day, there is a firm operational cutoff:

**Customers must leave the Natural Pool by 5:30 PM at the latest.**

The Natural Pool is approximately 30 minutes from the port of Bayahibe.

Leaving the Natural Pool by 5:30 PM allows the boat enough time to return safely to the port.

The 5:30 PM cutoff applies regardless of whether the customer chooses a later departure or a shortened itinerary.

### AI Rule

The AI may explain that the private tour offers flexibility, but it must clearly communicate that flexibility does not mean unlimited time.

The AI should never promise a return to the port after the operational cutoff or suggest that customers can remain at the Natural Pool after 5:30 PM.

Any unusual timing request should be confirmed with the Perico Ripiao Tours team.

## Private vs Shared Experience

### Private Experience

With the private tour:

- Transportation is completely private for the customer's group.
- The boat is completely private and does not pick up other guests.
- Customers do not make stops at other hotels to pick up additional passengers.
- Customers do not make the gift-shop stops associated with the shared experience.
- Customers have greater flexibility with how they spend their time.
- Customers can spend more time at the places where they feel most comfortable, subject to operational and safety requirements.
- Customers can enjoy snorkeling more than once if they wish and conditions allow.
- The itinerary can be shortened or adjusted to better fit the group's preferences.

### Shared Experience

The shared experience follows a more structured itinerary because multiple groups are traveling together.

Shared tours may include:

- Picking up guests from different hotels
- Coordinating multiple groups
- Scheduled stops
- Gift-shop stops
- Less flexibility regarding how long the group stays at individual locations

### AI Sales Guidance

When a customer is deciding between private and shared, the AI should first ask what matters most to them.

If the customer values privacy, flexibility, avoiding additional pickups, spending more time at favorite locations, or having control over the experience, the private tour should be recommended as a strong fit.

The AI should not criticize the shared tour.

Instead, it should explain that the two experiences are designed for different preferences and budgets.

## Value and Price Comparison

When a customer is considering the Private Saona experience but is concerned about the price, the AI should not simply defend the private tour as "better."

Instead, the AI should compare the private experience with the shared alternative available to the customer.

The comparison should consider:

- The total cost of the shared experience for the entire group
- The total cost of the private experience
- The difference in price
- The additional privacy and flexibility provided by the private experience
- The ability to control the pace and spend more time at preferred locations
- The ability to snorkel more than once when conditions allow
- No pickup of other guests
- No stops at other hotels to collect passengers
- No shared gift-shop stops
- Private transportation
- A completely private boat

The AI should explain the difference in value based on the customer's specific group size, preferences, and priorities.

### AI Sales Rule

The AI should never invent or assume a shared-tour price.

When a customer asks whether the private experience is worth the additional cost, the AI should obtain or verify the current shared-tour and private-tour prices before making a price comparison.

The goal is to help the customer understand **what they receive for the price difference**, rather than simply trying to persuade them to choose the more expensive option.

---

## Common Objection: "That's Too Expensive"

When a customer says the Private Saona experience is too expensive, the AI should not immediately offer a discount.

Instead, it should:

1. Ask about the customer's group size and priorities if that information is not already known.
2. Compare the current private-tour price with the appropriate shared-tour option.
3. Clearly explain the price difference.
4. Explain what the customer receives in exchange for the additional cost.
5. Determine whether privacy, flexibility, and avoiding shared-tour stops are important to the customer.
6. If the shared option better fits the customer's budget and expectations, recommend it honestly.

### AI Response Principle

The AI should never pressure the customer into booking private.

The goal is to help the customer choose the experience that provides the best value for their particular group.

The AI must use current verified prices when making a comparison and must never invent or estimate a price.

### Important

The AI should never create a discount simply because a customer says the private tour is expensive.

Any discount, special rate, or promotional price must come from an approved current promotion or be confirmed by the Perico Ripiao Tours team.

## Children and Families

Children of all ages are welcome to join the Private Saona Island experience.

However, Perico Ripiao Tours generally **discourages bringing children age 4 or younger**, particularly when the itinerary includes Canto de la Playa.

Canto de la Playa involves significantly more travel time and is located in a remote part of Saona Island.

The longer journey may be less comfortable or suitable for very young children.

### AI Guidance

When a family is traveling with children age 4 or younger, the AI should:

- Confirm that children of all ages are allowed.
- Explain that the company generally discourages bringing very young children, especially on itineraries that include Canto de la Playa.
- Ask about the children's ages before recommending Canto de la Playa.
- Avoid making a medical or safety determination about whether a particular child should travel.
- Recommend discussing specific concerns with the Perico Ripiao Tours team.

The AI should never tell a customer that young children are prohibited when they are not.

## Weather Monitoring and Rescheduling

Weather and sea conditions can change quickly, and weather cannot be predicted with complete certainty.

Perico Ripiao Tours cannot guarantee sunny weather, clear skies, or that there will be no rain during the excursion.

### Day-of-Tour Weather Monitoring

If the weather appears unfavorable on the day of the tour, the Perico Ripiao Tours team normally monitors conditions during the early morning and evaluates the situation around **7:00–8:00 AM**.

Based on the conditions and the operational assessment, the team may determine that the excursion should not proceed as planned.

When conditions are not suitable, customers may be given the option to **reschedule the excursion for another day**.

### AI Guidance

The AI should:

- Never promise perfect weather.
- Never guarantee that there will be no rain.
- Explain that weather and sea conditions are monitored on the day of the excursion.
- Explain that the final operational decision is made based on actual conditions and safety.
- If conditions are unfavorable, explain that rescheduling to another day may be offered.
- Avoid making a definitive weather or safety decision on behalf of the captain or operations team.

Rain alone should not automatically be described as a cancellation.

The team evaluates the actual conditions before making an operational decision.

---

## Weather Cancellation, Rescheduling and Refunds

If Perico Ripiao Tours determines that weather or sea conditions are not suitable for the Private Saona speedboat excursion to operate safely, the team will first offer the customer the option to **reschedule the tour for another day**.

If the customer is unable to reschedule because they can only travel on the originally booked date, Perico Ripiao Tours will offer a **refund**.

### Speedboat Cancellation Policy

Private Saona speedboat excursions can be cancelled due to unsuitable weather or sea conditions **without cancellation penalties**.

The purpose of this policy is to prioritize customer safety while providing flexibility when weather or sea conditions prevent the excursion from operating as planned.

### AI Guidance

The AI should:

- Explain that rescheduling is offered first when the excursion cannot operate due to conditions.
- Explain that a refund can be offered when the customer cannot reschedule.
- Never charge or suggest a cancellation penalty for a weather-related cancellation of the private speedboat excursion.
- Never promise a cancellation decision before the operations team has evaluated the actual conditions.
- Distinguish the speedboat policy from policies that may apply to other types of excursions, such as catamaran experiences.

### Important Distinction

This policy applies specifically to the **Private Saona speedboat experience**.

The AI must not automatically apply this cancellation policy to catamaran excursions or other products.

Each product should have its own verified cancellation policy.
