## Typical Experience

Guests are picked up from their hotel early in the morning and travel toward Santo Domingo in an air-conditioned vehicle.

Upon arrival, the group begins a guided exploration of the capital's historic and cultural landmarks. The itinerary includes the Colonial Zone, Las Damas Street, the First Cathedral of the Americas, and other important monuments connected to the early history of the Americas.

Because Alcázar de Colón is temporarily closed for maintenance, the current itinerary includes Fortaleza Ozama instead.

Guests also see the National Palace and Presidential Palace area, the Malecón, and modern landmarks such as the Fine Arts Palace and National Theatre.

Lunch is served at a typical Dominican restaurant, featuring traditional Dominican cuisine. After lunch, guests continue exploring the Colonial Zone and surrounding historical areas before returning to Punta Cana.

## Main Attractions

### Colonial Zone

The oldest historic district of Santo Domingo, known for its colonial architecture, museums, plazas, churches, and narrow historic streets.

### First Cathedral of the Americas

The Catedral Metropolitana Santa María de la Encarnación, recognized as the first cathedral established in the Americas.

### Las Damas Street

One of the oldest streets in the Americas, surrounded by important colonial monuments.

### Fortaleza Ozama

A historic military fortress in the Colonial Zone. It is currently included in place of Alcázar de Colón while the palace is closed for maintenance.

### National Palace and Presidential Palace Area

Guests enjoy panoramic views and may stop for photographs near the presidential palace area.

### Malecón de Santo Domingo

The city's waterfront boulevard, offering views of the Caribbean Sea and modern Santo Domingo.

### Faro a Colón

A major landmark associated with the 500th anniversary of Columbus's arrival in the Americas.
