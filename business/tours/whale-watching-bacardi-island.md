## Typical Experience

Guests are picked up from their hotel and travel by road toward the fishing town of Cañitas. The journey includes views of the Dominican countryside and the coastal mountain ranges.

At Cañitas, guests board a comfortable double-decker catamaran and sail across Samaná Bay toward the humpback whale sanctuary. During the whale-watching portion, guests search for humpback whales and other marine life.

The excursion continues to Cayo Levantado, where guests enjoy lunch on the beach and free time to swim, relax, and explore the island. Afterward, the group returns by boat to Cañitas and then travels back to Punta Cana.

## Whale-Watching Season

Humpback whales regularly visit Samaná Bay during their seasonal migration. The supplier identifies the main whale-watching period as approximately mid-January through mid-April.

Whale sightings are wildlife encounters and cannot be guaranteed.
