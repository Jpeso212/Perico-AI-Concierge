# PRODUCT SUMMARY

A private cultural experience combining Altos de Chavón with a visit to the Ron Barceló rum experience.

The excursion operates privately for the customer's group and includes private transportation, driver and guide.

Customers may select among different Ron Barceló experiences depending on their interests and group composition.

Available rum experience options:

1. Añejo Experience
2. Añejo PLUS
3. Imperial Experience
4. Optional Bartender Experience add-on

This is a multi-destination cultural route.

Altos de Chavón is in the La Romana area.

The Ron Barceló experience is located in San Pedro de Macorís.

Do NOT describe the Ron Barceló facility as being located in La Romana.
