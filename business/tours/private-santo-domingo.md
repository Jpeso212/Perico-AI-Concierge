# Private Santo Domingo Tour – Culture & Local Life

## Overview

The Private Santo Domingo Tour: Culture & Local Life is a full-day private excursion from Punta Cana to Santo Domingo, the capital of the Dominican Republic.

This experience is designed for guests who want to understand Santo Domingo beyond its famous landmarks. The tour combines Dominican history, culture, local cuisine, museums, historic neighborhoods, and everyday Dominican life at a comfortable pace.

There are no mandatory shopping or souvenir stops.
