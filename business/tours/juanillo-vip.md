# Juanillo VIP

## Overview

Experience an exclusive Scape Park adventure combining a luxury catamaran cruise, snorkeling, a natural freshwater cenote, lunch at Juanillo Beach Club, and leisure time on Juanillo Beach.

This premium experience begins at the marina in Cap Cana aboard a spacious double-decker catamaran. Enjoy a relaxing cruise through turquoise Caribbean waters, snorkel at a vibrant coral reef, and stop at a shallow sandbar surrounded by crystal-clear water.

After the cruise, enjoy lunch by the beach at Juanillo Beach Club, followed by free time to swim, relax, and enjoy Juanillo Beach. The day continues with a visit to Blue Hole, a hidden cenote surrounded by lush jungle and natural scenery.
