# PRODUCT SUMMARY

El Dorado Water Park is a full-day water park experience located in Cap Cana, Dominican Republic.

The park features more than 20 attractions combining water activities, adventure, relaxation and family entertainment.

Main experiences include:

- Water slides
- Wave pool
- Lazy river
- Lake kayaks
- Zip line
- Natural cenote
- Children's area
- Relaxation areas

The park is suitable for families, couples, groups and travelers looking for a full-day recreational experience in Punta Cana.
