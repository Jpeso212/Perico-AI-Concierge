## Typical Experience

The experience combines two separate activities.

The Ocean Runners portion departs from the Yacht Club of Cabeza de Toro and includes a catamaran cruise, snorkeling, and a relaxing stop at the Natural Pool.

The Jungle Buggies Adventures portion adds an off-road driving experience through natural and rural terrain.

The exact order of activities, transportation arrangements, departure times, and total duration should be confirmed when the reservation is made.
