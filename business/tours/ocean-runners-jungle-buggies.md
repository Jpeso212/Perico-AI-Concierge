## Product Summary

A combined Runners Adventures experience that brings together Ocean Runners and Jungle Buggies Adventures.

Guests enjoy the marine experience of Ocean Runners, including a catamaran cruise, snorkeling and a Natural Pool visit, together with the off-road Jungle Buggies experience.

This is a separate combination product.

Ocean Runners and Jungle Buggies Adventures also remain available as individual standalone excursions.

### Pricing Logic

- Single configuration: US$179 total.
- Double configuration: US$249 total for 2 people.
- Triple configuration: US$329 total for 3 people.
- Quadruple configuration: US$409 total for 4 people.
- These are the current Perico selling prices for this combination.
- The Double, Triple and Quadruple rates are total configuration prices, not per-person prices.
- Do not multiply the configuration price by the number of passengers.
- Do not quote the standalone Ocean Runners US$75 adult price as the price of this combination.
- Do not calculate the combination price by adding the standalone Ocean Runners and Jungle Buggies prices.
- Use the combination pricing table above when the customer books this product.
- Supplier website promotions, promotional codes, flash sales, early-booking discounts or direct-customer offers do not apply to Perico agency bookings unless Perico explicitly authorizes them.
