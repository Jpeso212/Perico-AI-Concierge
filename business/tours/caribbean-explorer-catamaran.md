## PRODUCT SUMMARY

The Caribbean Explorer Catamaran is a shared catamaran experience in Punta Cana combining sailing, snorkeling, swimming, music, drinks, and onboard entertainment.

The experience is designed for travelers who want a fun and social Caribbean boat excursion rather than a full-day destination tour such as Saona Island.

## ITINERARY

### 1. Hotel Pickup

Customer is picked up from an eligible hotel or designated meeting point in the Punta Cana area.

Exact pickup time depends on the customer's location.

### 2. Arrival and Boarding

Guests arrive at the departure point, meet the crew, receive safety instructions, and board the catamaran.

### 3. Coastal Sailing

The catamaran sails along the Punta Cana coastline.

Guests can enjoy music, drinks, Caribbean views, and the onboard atmosphere.

### 4. Snorkeling

The excursion includes a snorkeling stop.

Snorkeling equipment is provided.

### 5. Swimming / Water Stop

Guests have an opportunity to swim and enjoy the Caribbean water.

Exact location must be confirmed before the AI describes or promises a specific beach, reef, or natural pool.

### 6. Music, Drinks & Entertainment

Guests enjoy music, onboard entertainment, and national drinks during the experience.

### 7. Return

The catamaran returns to the departure point and guests are transported back to their applicable hotel or meeting point.
