## PRODUCT SUMMARY

Zipline Mega Splash is an adventure experience at Bávaro Adventure Park combining a 6-line zipline circuit, Himalayan hanging bridge, Mega Splash water landing, Blue Lagoon Cenote, Waterfall Pool, Dominican Village, Maze Garden, and traditional Dominican lunch.

The experience combines adrenaline, nature, swimming, and Dominican culture.

## MAIN EXPERIENCES

### 1. Zipline Mega Splash

Guests participate in a 6-line zipline circuit offering views of the Punta Cana landscape.

The course includes a Himalayan hanging bridge.

The final zipline features the Mega Splash experience with a water landing.

---

### 2. Blue Lagoon Cenote

Guests can enjoy an open freshwater cenote surrounded by tropical vegetation.

Swimming is available.

---

### 3. Waterfall Pool

Guests can swim and relax in the park's waterfall pool after the adventure activities.

---

### 4. Maze Garden

Access to the Maze Garden is included.

---

### 5. Dominican Village

Guests visit a recreation of a traditional Dominican village and learn about Dominican rural life and culture.

Coffee and cocoa sampling may be available.

---

### 6. Dominican Lunch

Traditional Dominican lunch is included.

The supplier currently describes the meal as:

- Rice
- Beans
- Oven-roasted chicken
- Creole-style sauce

Drinks with lunch are NOT included.
