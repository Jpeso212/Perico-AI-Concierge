# Product Summary

A 3-hour off-road adventure operated by Tours Point RD in Punta Cana.

Guests choose from several machine options, including ATV, Buggy, Family Buggy, Polaris, and Honda Pioneer.

The standard excursion route is:

**Ranch → Atabey → Macao Beach → Ranch**

The experience combines off-road driving, a visit to Atabey with local product tastings, and approximately 35 minutes at Macao Beach.

Atabey and Macao Beach are stops on the Tours Point excursion. Atabey is not the supplier or operator.

This product must remain completely separate from Adventure Boogies, Domitai Park, and other buggy excursions sold by Perico Ripiao Tours.
