# PRODUCT SUMMARY

Coco Bongo Punta Cana is a nightlife and entertainment experience combining a nightclub atmosphere with live performances, acrobatics, musical tributes, dancing and interactive entertainment.

This is not a conventional nightclub experience.

Customers attend an ongoing entertainment show combined with music, drinks and nightlife.

Perico Ripiao Tours currently offers multiple Coco Bongo ticket categories.
