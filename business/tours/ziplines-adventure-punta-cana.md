# Ziplines Adventure Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A half-day ziplining adventure through the tropical mountains of Punta Cana. Guests receive professional safety instruction before navigating a 12-cable circuit across 18 platforms, including an approximately 800-meter side-by-side zipline.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Anamuya Mountain / Punta Cana region, Dominican Republic

Duration: Approximately 5 hours

## Pricing

Prices are per person:

- Adult ages 13+: US$90
- Child ages 6–12: US$45
- Observer: US$45

## Zipline Course Details

- 12 cables.
- 18 platforms.
- Longest cable approximately 800 meters.
- Side-by-side cable available.
- Course extends across approximately 2 kilometers of mountain terrain.
- Guests are secured using climbing equipment and double-line cables.
- The course is certified annually by the Association for Challenge Course Technology (ACCT).

## Age Requirements

- Minimum age: 6 years old.
- Children ages 6–12 may participate only through zipline cable #8.
- Children must be accompanied by an adult.
- Adults are ages 13 and older.

## Physical Requirements

- Minimum weight: 20 kg / 44 lb.
- Maximum weight: 127 kg / 280 lb.
- Maximum waist measurement: 112 cm / 44 in.
- Covered shoes are required.
- Guests must attend the safety briefing.
- All participants must sign a liability waiver.

## Restrictions

- Pregnant guests cannot participate.
- Guests with significant mobility problems should not participate.
- Guests with heart conditions should confirm suitability before booking.
- Guests with back problems should confirm suitability before booking.
- Flip-flops are not recommended or permitted for the activity.
