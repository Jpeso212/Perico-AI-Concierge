## 5. Montaña Redonda

The excursion includes admission to Montaña Redonda.

Guests ascend Montaña Redonda aboard a safari-style truck.

At the summit, guests can enjoy panoramic views of areas including:

- Laguna Limón
- Laguna Redonda
- Samaná Bay
- Atlantic Ocean
