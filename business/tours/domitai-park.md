# Domitai Park

## Overview

Domitai Park is a full-day adventure and cultural experience in Macao, Punta Cana, combining adrenaline, Dominican traditions, nature, and entertainment.

Guests enjoy dune buggies, ziplining, horseback riding, cave exploration, a private cenote swim, organic product tastings, a buffet lunch, a visit to a Taíno village, and a Mardi Gras-style carnival show.

## Itinerary

### Morning Schedule

- 7:30–8:30 AM: Hotel pickup.
- 9:00 AM: Arrival at Domitai Park, check-in, and briefing with the guide.
- 9:15 AM–12:45 PM: Dune buggies, zipline, horseback riding, Yara Cave, and Taíno village activities.
- 1:00 PM: Buffet lunch.

- 2:30 PM: Visit to Iguanaboina Cave and cenote swim.
- 3:00 PM: Carnival show.
- 4:00 PM: Transfer back to the hotels.

## Important Information

- Children must be over 6 years old to participate in horseback riding and ziplining.
- Drivers must be over 18 years old.
- Family buggies for up to four passengers are available upon request.
- Not suitable for pregnant women.
- Not suitable for people with mobility issues.
- Guests weighing over 280 lb / 130 kg cannot participate in the zipline.
- Activity conditions and schedules may vary.
