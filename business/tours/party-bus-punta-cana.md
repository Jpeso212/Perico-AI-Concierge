# PRODUCT SUMMARY

Party Bus Punta Cana is a private group nightlife experience aboard a fully equipped party bus.

The vehicle becomes the group's private mobile party venue with music, drinks, club lighting and onboard entertainment features.

The experience is designed for private groups such as:

- Birthdays
- Bachelor parties
- Bachelorette parties
- Wedding groups
- Groups of friends
- Private celebrations
- Special events

This is a PRIVATE experience.

Unrelated customers are not mixed with the group.
