# Scape Park Full Admission

## Product Overview

A full-day adventure park experience in Cap Cana, Punta Cana. Scape Park combines outdoor adventure, freshwater attractions, caves, zip lines, ecological trails, cultural experiences, and controlled wildlife encounters in a natural tropical setting.

The Full Admission ticket provides access to the included attractions listed below.

## Included Attractions

### Blue Hole Cenote Trail

A scenic trail through varied ecosystems, including wetlands, a hanging bridge, waterfalls, and a hidden cenote.

### Cultural Route

A nature and cultural walk featuring replicas of homes associated with Taíno Indigenous communities, Spanish conquistadors, enslaved Africans, and Dominican peasants.

### Saltos Azules

An aquatic adventure featuring the Mabo rope, Macuya Swing, monkey bars, and water hammocks surrounded by caves and waterfalls.

### Zip Line Eco Splash

An aquatic zip-line experience combining aerial adventure with water fountains, cascades, and water cannons.

### Cave Expedition

An underground adventure through chambers and tunnels featuring geological formations.

### Monkey Playground

Controlled open spaces where guests can observe parrots, capuchin monkeys, and rhinoceros iguanas.

### Rope Runners

An aerial challenge course through the treetops featuring suspended ropes and obstacles.

### Super Ride Zip Line

A zip-line attraction designed to create the sensation of flying through the air.

## Typical Experience

Guests arrive at Scape Park and spend the day exploring the included attractions.

The park's attractions are connected by ecological trails and natural paths. Guests can combine cultural experiences, freshwater swimming, caves, wildlife observation, zip lines, and aerial adventure activities according to the day's attraction schedule.

Some attractions have limited operating hours, so guests should check the schedule before visiting.

## Requirements and Restrictions

- Children must meet the requirements of each individual attraction.
- Activities involving zip lines are not recommended for pregnant women or people with significant illness or disability.
- Park trails are in natural conditions and may present difficulties for strollers, cars, and guests with mobility limitations.
- Guests under the influence of alcohol or toxic substances cannot participate.
- Professional photo and video equipment is not permitted.
- Drones are not permitted.
- Maximum weight for zip-line activities: 280 lb.
- Minimum weight for most zip-line activities: 60 lb.
- Minimum weight for the Super Ride: 100 lb.
- Maximum height for zip-line activities: 6 ft 5 in.
- Minimum height for zip-line activities: 4 ft.
