# PRODUCT SUMMARY

A private group Montaña Redonda experience with three selectable options combining Montaña Redonda and Playa Limón with horseback riding, Four Wheels, or both activities.

This is the master Perico Ripiao Tours product for these Montaña Redonda private group experiences.

Do not create or recommend separate Classic, Regular, or Montaña Redonda-only versions.

Los Haitises + Montaña Redonda remains a separate product.

---

# OPERATING RULES

- Minimum: 8 paying guests
- Available any day of the week
- Subject to availability
- Private group experience
- Round-trip transportation included
- Guide included
- Hotel/accommodation must be collected to coordinate pickup
- Exact pickup and return times depend on hotel location and operations

If the group has fewer than 8 paying guests, do not automatically confirm or quote the standard per-person arrangement as bookable. The minimum is 8 paying guests.
