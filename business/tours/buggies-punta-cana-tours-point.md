# Buggies in Punta Cana with Macao Beach Stop – Tours Point RD

## Overview

Experience Punta Cana on an exciting off-road buggy adventure with Tours Point RD, including a stop at the famous Macao Beach.

This excursion combines countryside driving, off-road trails, and time at Macao Beach, one of the most popular natural beaches in the Punta Cana area.

Guests can choose from different vehicle experiences, including state-of-the-art dune buggies, ATV routes, and VIP rides featuring selected Honda, Can-Am, and Polaris vehicles.
