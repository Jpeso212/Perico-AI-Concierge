# Sanael Caribbean Boat – Private Boat & Events Punta Cana

## Overview

Sanael Caribbean Boat is a private trimaran experience in Bávaro–Punta Cana for groups, celebrations, weddings, receptions, birthdays, cocktails, business dinners, snorkeling trips, and other private events.

This is a standalone supplier product and must not be merged with the generic Private Party Boat product.

The standard experience is 3 hours aboard and sails along the Punta Cana/Bávaro coastline with a natural-pool stop. Extra hours may be added upon request and confirmation.

## Supplier

- Supplier: Sanael Caribbean Boat
- Location: Bávaro–Punta Cana
- Product type: Private boat / private events / group celebrations
- Minimum billing: 20 people
- Guests fewer than 20 are charged at the 20-person minimum.
- The supplier material describes capacity options of up to approximately 70–100 guests depending on configuration; final capacity must be confirmed before booking.

## Vessel Features

- Two-level trimaran.
- Lower level with kitchen, bar, spacious dance floor, and two bathrooms.
- Upper level with lounge sofas and open views.
- Beach boarding using two access ramps / hydraulic access system.
- Wheelchair accessible according to the supplier material.
- Tables, chairs, glasses, and cutlery available onboard.

## Duration and Operating Hours

- Standard experience: 3 hours aboard.
- Supplier operating window: 10:00 AM–6:00 PM with advance reservation.
- The boat must return to the beach before 6:00 PM.
- Extra hours may be requested and are subject to confirmation.

## Standard Experience

A typical Sanael private boat experience includes:

1. Boarding in the Bávaro–Punta Cana area.
2. Private cruise along the Punta Cana/Bávaro coastline.
3. DJ and music onboard.
4. Snorkeling.
5. Natural-pool stop.
6. Selected food/menu package.
7. National open bar according to the selected package.
8. Return to the beach before the supplier's operating cutoff.

Exact timing, route, snorkeling, and water activities remain subject to weather, sea conditions, and supplier confirmation.

## Supplier Reservation and Payment Policy

The supplied Sanael cancellation/payment policy states:

- 20% deposit of the total invoice is required to reserve and confirm the event.
- The supplier deposit is non-refundable after confirmation by company email or WhatsApp.
