## Typical Experience

Guests are picked up from their hotel and travel toward the Samaná region. The journey includes panoramic views of Miches and the Anamuya Mountains.

Upon reaching the departure area, guests board a luxury double-decker catamaran and sail across Samaná Bay in search of humpback whales.

The excursion continues to El Limón Waterfall, located in the heart of the Samaná Peninsula. Guests travel into the rainforest and continue toward the waterfall, where they can enjoy the natural surroundings and scenic beauty.

After the waterfall visit, guests enjoy lunch before beginning the return journey to Punta Cana.

## Whale-Watching Season

The supplier describes humpback whale watching as a seasonal natural event that takes place once a year during the Caribbean breeding season.

Whale sightings are wildlife encounters and cannot be guaranteed.

## El Limón Waterfall

El Limón Waterfall is approximately 165 feet high and is located in the rainforest of the Samaná Peninsula.

The visit involves traveling through natural terrain to reach the waterfall. Guests should be prepared for an outdoor excursion in a tropical environment.
