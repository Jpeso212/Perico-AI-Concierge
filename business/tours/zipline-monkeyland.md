# Zipline & Monkeyland Adventure Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A full-day adventure combining ziplining through the Dominican tropical forest, a visit to the Coconut House, and an interactive experience with squirrel monkeys at Monkeyland.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Punta Cana / Anamuya Mountains, Dominican Republic

Duration: Approximately 8 hours

Availability: Confirm current operating schedule before booking.

## Pricing

- Adult: US$130 per person
- Child ages 6–12: US$85 per person
- Observer: US$85 per person
- Promotional pricing may apply.

## Main Highlights

- 12 ziplines across 18 platforms.
- Longest zipline approximately 800 meters.
- Two of the longest zipline cables in the Dominican Republic and Caribbean.
- Approximately 45 minutes interacting with squirrel monkeys at Monkeyland.
- Visit to the Coconut House.
- Learn about local coconut oil production and countryside life.
- Taste locally produced coffee, coconut, and tropical fruits.
- Visit a typical Dominican countryside home and farm.
- Mountain and tropical forest scenery.

## Included

- Hotel pickup and drop-off.
- Professional guides.
- Zipline equipment.
- Safety orientation and training.
- Lunch.
- Water.
- Monkeyland visit.
- Coconut House and countryside experience.

## Requirements

- Minimum age: 6 years old.
- Children must be accompanied by an adult.
- Minimum weight: 20 kg / 44 lb.
- Maximum weight: 127 kg / 280 lb.
- Maximum waist measurement: 112 cm / 44 in.
- Covered shoes are required.
- All participants must sign a liability waiver.
- Children ages 6–12 only participate up to zipline #8.

## Restrictions

- Pregnant guests cannot participate.
- Guests with disabilities or mobility problems cannot participate.
- Not recommended for guests with back problems.
- Guests with a cold or flu cannot participate.
- Direct bookings from cruise-line passengers are not accepted; cruise passengers must book through their cruise line.
