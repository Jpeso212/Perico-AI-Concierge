# Saona Shared Tour

## Overview

Caribbean Saona offers a shared excursion to Saona Island seven days a week. The experience includes hotel transportation, marine transportation, beach time on Saona Island, a natural pool visit, lunch, drinks, and entertainment.

This is a shared group excursion and should not be presented as a private tour.

## Provider

- Provider: Caribbean Saona
- Tour type: Shared excursion
- Destination: Saona Island, Dominican Republic
- Availability: 7 days a week
- Hotel pickup and return transportation included

## Transportation

The excursion uses both a catamaran and a motomarán during the marine transportation portion.

- Outbound transportation may be either a catamaran or a motomarán.
- Return transportation uses the other vessel.
- Example: Catamaran outbound and motomarán return, or motomarán outbound and catamaran return.
- The exact vessel arrangement depends on the daily operation and is confirmed by Caribbean Saona.

## Marine Transportation Time

The catamaran journey takes approximately 2–2.5 hours.

This timing may apply to either direction:

- If the catamaran is used outbound, the outbound journey takes approximately 2–2.5 hours.
- If the catamaran is used for the return, the return journey takes approximately 2–2.5 hours.
- The other direction is operated by motomarán.

The exact transportation schedule may vary depending on the daily operating arrangement and sea conditions.

## Duration

- Total excursion duration: Approximately 11 hours, including transportation.
- Time on Saona Island beach: Approximately 3 hours.
- Natural pool visit: Approximately 45 minutes.
- Catamaran journey: Approximately 2–2.5 hours, whether used outbound or on the return journey.

Actual timing may vary depending on transportation logistics, sea conditions, and the daily operating schedule.

## Activities and Experience

The shared Saona excursion generally follows this structure:

1. Hotel pickup and transportation to the departure point.
2. Marine transportation using either a catamaran or motomarán.
3. Entertainment and animation during the catamaran portion.
4. Arrival at Saona Island.
5. Approximately 3 hours of beach time.
6. Lunch and open bar service.
7. Visit to the natural pool for approximately 45 minutes.
8. Return marine transportation using the other vessel.
9. Hotel drop-off.

The exact sequence and timing may vary according to the daily operating schedule.
