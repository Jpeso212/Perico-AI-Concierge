# PRODUCT SUMMARY

Catalina Island Scuba Diving is a full-day dive excursion from the Punta Cana / Bávaro area for certified divers.

The experience combines:

- Hotel transportation
- Boat transportation
- 2 guided scuba dives
- Catalina Island
- The Wall
- The Aquarium
- Beach / surface interval
- Lunch
- Alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks
- Complete dive equipment
- Professional dive guides

Catalina Island is known for its clear Caribbean water, coral formations and dramatic underwater wall.

The supplier identifies The Wall as the signature dive of this excursion.
