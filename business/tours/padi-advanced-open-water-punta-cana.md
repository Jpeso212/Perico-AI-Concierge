# PRODUCT SUMMARY

The PADI Advanced Open Water Diver Course is designed for already-certified Open Water Divers who want to expand their diving skills, experience different types of diving and increase their certified depth limit.

The current Grand Bay program includes:

- 5 Adventure Dives
- Deep Adventure Dive
- Underwater Navigation Adventure Dive
- 3 additional Adventure Dives
- PADI professional instruction
- Complete scuba equipment
- PADI eLearning / knowledge development
- Small-group training
- Hotel transportation

The course is primarily experience-based.

Students learn by diving under instructor supervision rather than repeating beginner-level confined-water training.

After successful completion of all required dives and course requirements, the diver earns the PADI Advanced Open Water Diver certification.

# COMPARISON LOGIC

## Open Water vs Advanced Open Water

PADI OPEN WATER:

- Beginner certification
- No prior certification required
- Knowledge development
- Confined-water training
- 4 open-water certification dives
- Maximum certification depth 18 m / 60 ft

PADI ADVANCED OPEN WATER:

- Intermediate certification
- Open Water certification required
- 5 Adventure Dives
- Deep Dive required
- Underwater Navigation required
- 3 additional Adventure Dives
- Maximum certification depth 30 m / 100 ft

An Adventure Dive completed during Advanced Open Water is NOT automatically the same as completing the full corresponding PADI Specialty certification.

Examples:

Wreck Adventure Dive
≠
PADI Wreck Diver Specialty certification

≠
PADI Deep Diver Specialty certification
