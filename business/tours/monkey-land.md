# Monkeyland Punta Cana

## Product Overview

Monkeyland Punta Cana is a guided cultural and wildlife excursion operated by Runners Adventures.

The experience combines a visit to a typical Dominican country house, an introduction to local agricultural products, a botanical garden visit, and guided interaction with squirrel monkeys.

The tour is suitable for guests interested in nature, Dominican culture, wildlife, and family-friendly experiences, subject to the operator's participation restrictions.

Perico Ripiao Tours offers the excursion with or without transportation.

## Main Itinerary

The itinerary follows the operator's standard Monkeyland experience. Exact timing may vary according to the selected departure, transportation arrangements, and operational conditions.

### 1. Hotel Pickup

For bookings that include transportation, guests are picked up from their hotel.

Guests without transportation must arrange their own arrival according to the confirmed booking instructions.

### 2. Visit to a Typical Dominican Country House

Guests visit a typical Dominican country house and learn about local culture and traditional ways of living.

### 3. Plantation Experience

The experience includes an introduction to organic agricultural products traditionally associated with the Dominican Republic.

Guests may learn about and sample products such as:

- Coffee.
- Cocoa.

### 4. Botanical Garden

Guests visit a botanical garden featuring native plants and flowers.

This portion of the experience provides an opportunity to learn more about the local natural environment.

### 5. Monkeyland Experience

Guests participate in a guided interaction with squirrel monkeys.

The Monkeyland portion generally lasts approximately 45 minutes, subject to the operator's schedule and conditions.

Guests should follow all instructions provided by the guides during the interaction.

### 6. Gift Shop

The experience includes a visit to the Monkeyland gift shop.

Guests may browse available souvenirs and products.

### 7. Return Transportation

For bookings with transportation, guests are returned to their hotel after completing the excursion.
