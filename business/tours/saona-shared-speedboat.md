## Product Summary

Shared full-day Saona Island excursion traveling by motomarán or
speedboat from Bayahibe.

This itinerary is designed for travelers who want to explore more of
Saona Island than a standard beach-only excursion.

The experience includes Playa de los Flamencos, Mano Juan, Canto de la
Playa, the Natural Pools, and a panoramic passage through the Catuano
Canal and mangrove area.

This is a SHARED excursion.

It must not be confused with:

- Private Saona Speedboat
- Private Saona Catamaran
- Shared Saona Catamaran
