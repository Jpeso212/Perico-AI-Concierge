# Dolphin Explorer Downtown — Explorer Program

## Overview

Dolphin Explorer Downtown offers a guided swimming and interaction experience with one dolphin in Punta Cana, Dominican Republic.

Guests can hold hands with the dolphin, learn how to cue it, watch it leap over them, and enjoy kissing, dancing, and the belly ride.

## Group Information

- Maximum of 12 participants per group.
- One dolphin is assigned per group.

## Age, Height, and Swimming Requirements

- Children must be at least 1 meter tall.
- Children and adults must know how to swim.
- The program is designed for swimmers at an intermediate level or higher.

## Pregnancy and Health

- Pregnant guests are not permitted to participate.
- Guests with health conditions or physical disabilities should contact the operator before booking.
