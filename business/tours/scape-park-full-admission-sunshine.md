## Included Attractions

### Sunshine Cruise

Visit the marina area and cruise aboard a spacious catamaran. The experience includes snorkeling at Buccaneers' Reef followed by time to relax and enjoy a cold drink at a sandbar in crystal-clear waters.

### Blue Hole Cenote Trail

A scenic trail through varied ecosystems, including wetlands, a hanging bridge, waterfalls, and a hidden cenote.

### Cultural Route

A nature and cultural walk featuring replicas of homes associated with Taíno Indigenous communities, Spanish conquistadors, enslaved Africans, and Dominican peasants.

### Saltos Azules

An aquatic adventure featuring the Mabo rope, Macuya Swing, monkey bars, and water hammocks surrounded by caves and waterfalls.

### Zip Line Eco Splash

An aquatic zip-line experience combining aerial adventure with water fountains, cascades, and water cannons.

### Cave Expedition

An underground adventure through chambers and tunnels featuring geological formations.

### Monkey Playground

Controlled open spaces where guests can observe parrots, capuchin monkeys, and rhinoceros iguanas.

### Rope Runners

An aerial challenge course through the treetops featuring suspended ropes and obstacles.

### Super Ride Zip Line

A zip-line attraction designed to create the sensation of flying through the air.

## Typical Experience

Guests arrive at Scape Park and enjoy the included attractions according to the day's schedule.

The Sunshine Cruise provides a break from the park's land-based activities. Guests board a catamaran, cruise through Caribbean waters, and stop at Buccaneers' Reef for snorkeling. The experience continues in a relaxed atmosphere at a sandbar.

Guests then return to the park's adventure attractions, which may include freshwater swimming, caves, cultural trails, wildlife observation, zip lines, and aerial challenge courses.

Some attractions have limited operating hours, so guests should check the daily schedule before visiting.
