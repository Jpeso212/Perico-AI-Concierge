## PRODUCT SUMMARY

Buggy VIP is an off-road adventure at Bávaro Adventure Park combining buggy driving through a private tropical route with an off-road racetrack located in an abandoned mine.

The experience also includes Blue Lagoon Cenote, Dominican Village, Waterfall Pool, Maze Garden, and a traditional Dominican lunch.

Vehicles accommodate up to 4 people.

## MAIN EXPERIENCES

### 1. Buggy Adventure

Guests drive through Bávaro Adventure Park's private off-road route surrounded by forest and tropical vegetation.

The buggy experience includes an off-road racetrack located in an abandoned mine.

Vehicles can accommodate up to 4 people.

---

### 2. Blue Lagoon Cenote

Guests visit the park's freshwater Blue Lagoon Cenote.

Swimming is included as part of the experience.

---

### 3. Dominican Village

Guests visit a recreation of a traditional Dominican village and experience elements of Dominican rural culture.

---

### 4. Waterfall Pool

Guests can enjoy the park's Waterfall Pool after the adventure activities.

---

### 5. Maze Garden

Access to the Maze Garden is included.

---

### 6. Dominican Lunch

A traditional Dominican lunch is included.

Drinks with lunch are NOT included.
