# Dolphins Funtastic — Dolphin Interaction

## Overview

Funtastic is a dolphin interaction experience at Dolphin Explorer in Punta Cana, Dominican Republic.

Guests do not need to be expert swimmers. The experience includes an introductory dolphin interaction, a dolphin pec shake, a dolphin kiss, information about dolphin anatomy, and conservation education.
