# Private Party Boat – Punta Cana

## Overview

The Private Party Boat is a private catamaran experience in Punta Cana designed for families, friends, celebrations, and private groups who want their own boat, flexible scheduling, music, drinks, snorkeling, and a fun Caribbean atmosphere.

The experience includes a private catamaran, round-trip transportation, snorkeling, a natural pool, snacks, drinks, music, and animation. A water slide can be requested and must be confirmed before booking.

Optional services such as lunch, premium drinks, DJ service, decorations, and professional photography can be added at an additional cost.

## Price

- Starting price: US$500 total per private boat.
- The price is per boat, not per person.
- Final pricing depends on the number of guests, boat size, selected services, and requested extras.
- Private catamarans are available for groups of different sizes. Boat selection depends on the number of guests.

## Duration and Schedule

- Total excursion duration: approximately 4–5 hours, including transportation.
- Available time slots:
  - 9:00 AM–12:00 PM
  - 12:00 PM–3:00 PM
  - 3:00 PM–6:00 PM
- Exact departure time and itinerary may vary depending on the supplier, weather, sea conditions, and operational availability.

- A water slide is available upon request.
- The water slide is not automatically guaranteed.
- Perico Ripiao Tours must confirm that a suitable catamaran equipped with a water slide is available before promising it to the customer.

## Optional Services

Available at additional cost upon request:

- Lunch.
- Premium drinks or upgraded liquor.
- DJ service.
- Decorations.
- Professional photographer.
- Special celebration arrangements.

Lunch can be arranged, but it is generally not recommended for the standard three-hour boat schedule unless the group specifically requests it.

## Itinerary

A typical private Party Boat experience may include:

1. Hotel pickup and transportation to the departure point.
2. Boarding the private catamaran.
3. Sailing along the Punta Cana coastline.
4. Music, animation, and onboard drinks.
5. Snorkeling stop, subject to sea conditions.
6. Natural pool stop.
7. Time to enjoy the water and snacks.
8. Optional water slide experience, if requested and confirmed.
9. Return to the departure point.
10. Transportation back to the hotel.

The exact route, departure point, and sequence of activities may vary according to the assigned supplier and sea conditions.

## Children and Accessibility

- Children are allowed.
- Guests should be comfortable boarding and exiting a boat.
- Guests should be able to participate safely in water-based activities.
- Life jackets are available for safety.
- Guests with mobility limitations should contact Perico Ripiao Tours before booking so the appropriate boat and boarding arrangements can be evaluated.

## Weather and Sea Conditions

- Snorkeling and swimming activities depend on weather and sea conditions.
- The supplier or maritime authorities may modify or cancel the service when operating conditions are unsafe.
- Rain alone does not necessarily cancel the excursion.
- The itinerary may be adjusted for safety reasons.

## Cancellation and Deposit Policy

- A 50% deposit is required to confirm the private catamaran reservation.
- Cancellations made more than 72 hours before departure qualify for a 100% refund of all amounts paid.
- Cancellations made within 72 hours of departure are subject to the non-refundable 50% deposit.
- Once the reservation enters the 72-hour cancellation window, the deposit cannot be refunded.
- Cancellation or suspension due to an official government or maritime authority announcement closing tours for that day will be handled according to the supplier's operating and refund policy.
- Customers should contact Perico Ripiao Tours as soon as possible if they need to cancel or modify a reservation.
