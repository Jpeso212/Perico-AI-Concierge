# Sky Runners Helicopter Tours Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A shared helicopter sightseeing experience over Punta Cana and the Caribbean coastline. Guests enjoy panoramic aerial views of beaches, turquoise waters, resorts, marinas, and natural landscapes.

Supplier: Runners Adventures / Sky Runners

Location: Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

Flight Types:

- Arena Tour: 10 minutes
- Arrecife Tour: 15 minutes
- Coral Tour: 20 minutes
- Cana Tour: 30 minutes

All flights are shared.

## Pricing

Prices are per person:

- Arena Tour – 10 minutes: US$139
- Arrecife Tour – 15 minutes: US$190
- Coral Tour – 20 minutes: US$249
- Cana Tour – 30 minutes: US$359

Prices and availability must be confirmed before booking.

## Arena Tour – 10 Minutes

The shortest scenic flight focuses on Punta Cana's white-sand coastline and surrounding beaches.

Highlights:

- Caribbean coastline.
- White-sand beaches.
- Aerial views of Punta Cana's coastal scenery.
- Views of beaches that may be difficult to access from land.

## Arrecife Tour – 15 Minutes

A scenic flight over the reef and coastline surrounding Punta Cana.

Highlights:

- Bavaro.
- Punta Cana.
- Macao Beach.
- Turquoise Caribbean waters.
- Aerial views of hotel areas and beaches.
- Opportunity for memorable photographs from the helicopter.

## Coral Tour – 20 Minutes

An extended coastal flight over Punta Cana and the eastern Dominican Republic.

Highlights:

- Caribbean coastline.
- Nest Point, separating the Atlantic Ocean and Caribbean Sea.
- Bavaro Lake, a wildlife sanctuary.
- Coastal landscapes and natural waterways.
- Aerial views of beaches, lagoons, and vegetation.

## Cana Tour – 30 Minutes

The longest helicopter experience, covering Cap Cana, Cabeza de Toro, Punta Cana, Bavaro, and the coastline.

Highlights:

- Cap Cana Marina.
- Luxury coastal homes and resorts.
- Cabeza de Toro fishing village.
- Areas where dolphins may be visible from the air.
- Punta Cana and the Coco Bongo Dome.
- Bavaro beaches and hotels.
- Scenic return flight along the coastline.

## Included

- Hotel pickup in a comfortable HeliShuttle.
- Briefing at the heliport lounge.
- Narrated flight.
- Return transportation to the hotel.

## Important Flight Information

- Maximum passenger weight: 250 lb.
- Flights are shared.
- Pilots assign seats based on helicopter occupancy and required weight distribution.
- Seat selection cannot be guaranteed.
- Passengers should bring their booking voucher.
- Children under 18 must fly with an accompanying adult.
- If a minor is not flying with an adult, an adult must still be present at the heliport.

## Restrictions and Operational Rules

- Maximum weight limit is 250 lb per passenger.
- Guests who miss their pickup may lose their flight without a refund.
- Confirm passenger weight requirements before booking.
- Confirm hotel pickup location and pickup time.
- Confirm availability and flight schedule before promising a departure.
- Do not guarantee a specific seat.
- Do not guarantee a specific aircraft.
- Do not promise that dolphins or specific landmarks will be visible during the flight.
- Confirm current cancellation and refund terms before finalizing the reservation.

## Cancellation Policy

- Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the scheduled flight.
- Missing the pickup may result in loss of the flight and no refund.
