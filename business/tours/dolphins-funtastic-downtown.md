# Dolphins Funtastic Downtown — Dolphin Interaction

## Overview

Dolphins Funtastic Downtown is a dolphin interaction experience at Dolphin Explorer Downtown, Dominican Republic.

Guests participate in a guided interaction with dolphins and learn about dolphin behavior, anatomy, and conservation.

## Pricing

- Adult, age 13+: US$120 per person
- Child, ages 1–12: US$120 per person
- Promotional prices may start at US$108 per person, subject to availability.

## Duration

- Approximately 2 hours total.
- Includes approximately 40 minutes of water activities.
- Guests should arrive 30 minutes before the scheduled program.

## Schedule

Available daily, subject to availability.

Typical sessions:

- 9:30 AM
- 11:30 AM
- 1:30 PM

Confirm the schedule before booking.

- Maximum of 20 participants per group.
- One dolphin is assigned per group.

## Age and Swimming Requirements

- Minimum age: 1 year old.
- Children under 8 years old must be accompanied by an adult.
- Suitable for beginners and expert swimmers.
- Children under 1 meter tall may swim free when accompanied by a paying adult, subject to operator terms.

## Pregnancy and Health

- Pregnancy is allowed up to 6 months.
- Guests with medical conditions, mobility limitations, or disabilities should contact the operator before booking.
