# Punta Cana Zipline Adventure – Tours Point RD

## Overview

Get ready for an exhilarating zipline experience in the heart of Punta Cana with Tours Point RD.

This adventure includes four dynamic zipline jumps from heights reaching approximately 5,000 feet. Each zipline offers a different challenge, with lengths ranging from approximately 600 feet to 1,400 feet.

The experience combines aerial adventure, tropical scenery, and the excitement of flying above the Punta Cana landscape.

## Highlights

- Four zipline jumps
- Heights reaching approximately 5,000 feet
- Zipline lengths from approximately 600 to 1,400 feet
- Double steel cable system

- Guided adventure experience
