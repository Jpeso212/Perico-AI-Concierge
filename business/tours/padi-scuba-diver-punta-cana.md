## PADI Scuba Diver vs PADI Open Water Diver

PADI Scuba Diver:

- 2 days
- 2 open-water dives
- Maximum 12 m / 40 ft
- Requires professional supervision after certification

PADI Open Water Diver:

- More complete beginner certification
- Additional training
- 4 open-water dives
- Standard maximum depth 18 m / 60 ft
- Allows diving with a certified buddy within certification limits

AI should ask how much time the customer has and what type of certification they want.

Do not describe one certification as "better."
