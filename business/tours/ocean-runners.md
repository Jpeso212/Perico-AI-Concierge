# Ocean Runners Punta Cana

## Product Overview

A half-day shared catamaran excursion departing from Cabeza de Toro Yacht Club. The experience combines a relaxing coastal cruise, snorkeling, music, drinks, and time at a natural pool.

Supplier: Runners Adventures

Location: Yacht Club of Cabeza de Toro, Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

Duration: Approximately 4 hours

## Pricing

Prices are per person:

- Adult ages 13+: US$75
- Child ages 3–12: US$50

Early booking promotion:

- Promo code EARLY10 may provide 10% off when booking 10 or more days in advance.
- Promotional pricing is subject to supplier terms and availability.
