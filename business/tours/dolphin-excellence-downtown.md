# Royal Excellence Downtown — Swim with 2 Dolphins

## Overview

Royal Excellence Downtown is an advanced dolphin swimming and interaction experience at Dolphin Explorer Downtown in Punta Cana, Dominican Republic.

Guests receive a personalized introduction to the dolphins and are coached by trainers for advanced activities, including the famous foot push and dorsal ride with two dolphins.

## Experience Includes

- Personalized introduction to the dolphins
- Trainer-guided dolphin interaction
- Foot push experience

## Group Information

- Maximum of 10 participants per group.
- Two dolphins are assigned per group.

## Age, Height, and Swimming Requirements

- Children must be at least 1 meter tall.
- Children and adults must know how to swim.
- Designed for intermediate-level swimmers and above.

## Pregnancy and Health

- Pregnant guests are not permitted to participate.
- Guests with health conditions or physical disabilities should contact the operator before booking.
