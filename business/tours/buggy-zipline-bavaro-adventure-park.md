## PRODUCT SUMMARY

Buggy & Zipline is a combination adventure at Bávaro Adventure Park that brings together off-road buggy driving and the Zipline Mega Splash experience.

Guests drive through the park's private off-road circuit, including a route through an abandoned mine, and then experience a 6-line zipline circuit featuring the Himalayan Bridge and Mega Splash water landing.

The package also includes Blue Lagoon Cenote, Waterfall Pool, Maze Garden, Dominican Village and a traditional Dominican lunch.
