# Horseback Riding in Punta Cana Countryside – Tours Point RD

## Overview

Discover the natural beauty of the Dominican countryside on a guided horseback riding adventure with Tours Point RD.

Unlike beach horseback riding excursions, this experience focuses on inland trails and countryside scenery, offering guests a more rural and nature-oriented experience away from the resort areas.

## Highlights

- Guided horseback riding through the Dominican countryside
- Inland natural surroundings
- Scenic rural trails
- Relaxed outdoor adventure
- Experience away from the beach and resort areas
- Multiple departure times available

## Important Distinction

This is a countryside horseback riding experience.

It should not be confused with the separate Horseback Riding on the Beach excursion, which includes riding along or near the coastline.
