## Location
Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

## Product Summary
Shared deep-sea fishing excursion operated by Gone Fishing Punta Cana.

This is a 4-hour trolling experience with transportation from the confirmed Bavaro–Punta Cana hotel area, fishing equipment, bait, beverages, crew and guide support.

Guests may participate as fishermen or observers.

# MARINE AND WEATHER CONDITIONS

Deep-sea fishing is subject to marine and weather safety conditions.

Rain alone does not automatically cancel the excursion.

Unsafe sea conditions, excessive wind, tropical weather or other marine safety conditions may result in:

- Cancellation
- Postponement
- Rescheduling
- Route changes
- Operational modifications

The captain/operator has final authority regarding safe operation at sea.

---

# SEASICKNESS

Deep-sea fishing can involve significant boat movement.

Guests, especially families with children, should understand that seasickness is possible.

Once the boat has departed, seasickness does not automatically create a refund entitlement.

A shared boat does not automatically return to port because one passenger becomes seasick.

Route, continuation and return decisions remain under captain/operator authority.
