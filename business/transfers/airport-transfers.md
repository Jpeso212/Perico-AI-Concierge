\## Service



Private Airport \& Ground Transfers



\## Operator



Perico Ripiao Tours



\## Status

\## Service Overview



Perico Ripiao Tours provides private airport and ground transportation in the Dominican Republic.



Transfer prices are based on:



\- Origin.

\- Destination zone.

\- Number of passengers.

\- One-way or round-trip service.



Prices shown in this file are total transfer prices for the applicable passenger bracket, not per-person prices.



If an exact approved rate is not available, the Concierge must request a quote.



\---



\# Booking Information Required



Before quoting or booking a transfer, determine:



1\. Pickup airport or location.

2\. Hotel, resort, Airbnb, address or destination.

3\. Number of passengers.

4\. One-way or round trip.

5\. Travel date.

6\. Airline and flight number when applicable.

7\. Arrival or departure time when applicable.

8\. Special luggage or transportation requirements when applicable.



Never estimate a transfer price based on distance or a nearby destination.

\- Miches is a separate zone.

\- Casa de Campo must not automatically inherit La Romana pricing.

\- Playa Nueva Romana must not automatically inherit La Romana pricing.

If the customer's property cannot be confidently assigned to a zone, ask for the exact hotel, resort, Airbnb or address.

\# One-Way and Round-Trip Rules



\## One Way



Use the One Way column for a transfer in one direction.



\## Round Trip



Use the Round Trip column when the applicable transfer service includes both directions.

Use the published Round Trip rate.



\---



\# Passenger Pricing Rules



Rates are based on passenger brackets.



Example:



A group of 7 passengers uses the 5–7 passenger bracket.



The Concierge must not:



\- Multiply the group price by 7.

\- Divide the rate into a per-person price and reconstruct it.

\- Use multiple smaller passenger brackets to price one group.



If the group exceeds the maximum published bracket:



\*\*QUOTE REQUIRED\*\*

\- Arrival airport.

\- Arrival date.

\- Flight number.

\- Scheduled arrival time.

\- Hotel or destination.

\- Number of passengers.

\- Contact information when required.



\---



\# Airport Departure Requirements



For an airport departure transfer, collect:



\- Lead passenger name.

\- Pickup hotel or address.

\- Departure airport.

\- Departure date.

\- Airline.

\- Flight number.

\- Scheduled flight departure time.

\- Number of passengers.

\- Contact information when required.



Do not invent a pickup time.



The appropriate pickup time must be established before confirmation.



\---



\# Round-Trip Requirements



For a round-trip airport reservation, collect both flight segments.



\### Arrival



\- Arrival date.

\- Airline.

\- Flight number.

\- Arrival time.



\### Departure



\- Departure date.

\- Airline.

\- Flight number.

\- Departure time.



Also collect:



\- Hotel/property.

\- Passenger count.

\- Lead passenger name.

\- Contact information when required.



\---



\# Additional Stops



Published rates must not automatically be interpreted as including extra stops.



If the customer requests:



\- Multiple hotels.

\- Multiple pickup points.

\- Multiple drop-offs.

\- Shopping stops.

\- Additional scheduled stops.



verify the price before confirmation.



Do not invent an additional-stop charge.



\---



\# Special Transportation Requirements



When applicable, ask about:



\- Excessive luggage.

\- Oversized luggage.

\- Golf equipment.

\- Wheelchairs or mobility equipment.

\- Child transportation requirements.

\- Large-group requirements.

\- Multiple pickup/drop-off locations.



Never promise special equipment until availability is confirmed.

\- Use only approved Perico transfer prices.
